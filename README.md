# AxeSentry — Bitaxe monitor for iPhone

<a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/axesentry/id6781810919"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_on_the-App_Store-0D96F6?style=for-the-badge&logo=apple&logoColor=white" alt="Download on the App Store"></a>

Monitor and control your **Bitaxe, NerdAxe, NerdQAxe and NMAxe** miners over your local
Wi-Fi — hashrate, temperatures, alerts, solo block odds and one-tap pool switching.

No account, no cloud — nothing leaves your network.

## Features

- Dashboard for your whole swarm, with fleet health and per-miner history
- Alerts for overheating, low hashrate, offline miners, shares and blocks
- Auto-recovery: scheduled reboots and reboot-on-low-hashrate per miner
- Tuning: frequency, voltage and fan, plus primary/fallback pool switching
- Solo block odds, Live Activity and Home Screen widget

## Privacy

AxeSentry talks only to the miners on your own network (the AxeOS API). No analytics, no
servers. [Privacy policy](https://shiftingeden.github.io/axesentry-site/privacy-policy.html)
