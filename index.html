How it works

Built around AxeOS

Every Bitaxe-family miner exposes the same local API. AxeSentry speaks it natively, so setup takes seconds.

Scan or add by IP

AxeSentry finds AxeOS devices on your Wi-Fi in seconds.

Features

Small app, serious monitoring

Everything a Bitaxe owner checks in the AxeOS web page, without opening a browser tab for each device.

Swarm dashboard

Live hashrate, temperature, power, best share and uptime for every miner.

  • Swarm totals and graphs
  • List or grid view, sortable
  • Problem miners pinned to the top

Alerts that matter

Global or per-device thresholds, 4/8/12-hour mute, and in-app alert history.

  • High temp and low hashrate
  • Miners offline
  • New best share and block found

Auto-recovery

Opt-in reboots for miners that cross your limits, up to three attempts.

  • Undervolt/downclock on 3rd attempt if overheating
  • Scheduled reboots per miner
  • Low-hashrate reboot

Solo block odds

Your swarm's odds of hitting a block using live BTC or BCH difficulty, plus a full-screen celebration if it happens.

One-tap pool switching

Flip one miner or the whole swarm between Primary and Fallback pools.

Tuning

Frequency, core voltage and fan speed, per device or all at once, plus reboot and identify.

Widget & Live Activity

Glance at your swarm from the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

iCloud sync

Universal iPhone and iPad app; your miner list syncs privately through your iCloud.

Background checks

Best-effort monitoring while the app is closed, within iOS limits.

Safety · Security · Privacy

Nothing leaves your network.

AxeSentry talks to your miners over the local AxeOS API. That's the only connection it makes to your devices: no cloud relay and no developer server.

The only outside request is public network difficulty for block odds; it carries none of your data.

Support

Questions? I answer every email.

Email TechBitsMix@gmail.com for help, bug reports or feature requests.

My miners aren't showing up.

Make sure your iPhone or iPad is on the same Wi-Fi network as your miners, and allow local-network access when iOS asks. Then use Scan, or add a miner by its IP address.

Which devices are supported?

Bitaxe, NerdAxe/NerdQAxe and NMAxe miners running the AxeOS API.

Do alerts work when the app is closed?

Yes, on a best-effort basis. iOS controls how often background checks run, so alerts may be delayed. Keep Notifications and Background App Refresh on for AxeSentry.

Is auto-recovery safe?

It is off until you enable it, stops after three attempts, and only uses the same settings AxeOS offers in its web interface.

Does AxeSentry collect my data?

No. It works entirely on your local network with no account and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
